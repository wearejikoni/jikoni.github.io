# JIKONI

**CONNECT · CREATE · CELEBRATE**

JIKONI is a place where people, nature and ideas come together.

A place rooted in community, creativity, culture and nature.

## CONNECT

Connect with people.  
Connect with nature.  
Connect with yourself.

JIKONI brings people together — to share experiences, stories, knowledge and time.

## CREATE

Create with your hands.  
Create new ideas.  
Create something meaningful.

From traditional craft and natural building to retreats, projects and new ways of living together.

## CELEBRATE

Celebrate life.  
Celebrate culture.  
Celebrate community.

Good food, fire, music, stories, laughter and the simple joy of being together.

---

We believe meaningful change starts with connection, grows through creation and becomes something worth celebrating.
